Core Gameplay - Actions:
  ✅ Seek Investors (available once every 3 turns)
     - Shows 3 investor options
     - Each option: investor takes X% equity, gives Y valuation boost
     - Offers scale with current valuation
     - Creates new shares (dilutes player ownership)
  ✅ Skip Turn - advance to next turn without action
  ✅ Analyze - view detailed stats:
     - Market cap
     - Valuation  
     - Total share count
     - Player ownership %
     - Share price (estimated if private, real if public)
  ✅ Go IPO (only when private)
     - Player chooses what % of shares to offer publicly
     - Minimum: 10%
     - Game ends if total outside ownership exceeds 100%
     - After IPO, share price becomes "real" based on valuation ÷ shares

Events System:
  ✅ One event per turn after player's action
  ✅ Events are multiple choice (3-4 choices each)
  ✅ Some events are purely negative but choices affect severity
  ✅ Some events can be positive based on good choices
  ✅ All events affect the stock price/valuation
  ✅ 15+ unique events implemented

Expectations (Public Companies Only):
  ✅ Every 10 turns, there's an expected stock price
  ✅ Calculation based on:
     - Difficulty level (harder = higher expectations)
     - Past performance
  ✅ If actual stock price is below expected: stock price drops as penalty
  ✅ If at or above: no penalty
